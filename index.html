How the control law works

  • Slip ratio on acceleration: slip = (wheel_speed - vehicle_speed) / max(wheel_speed, 2 m/s)
  • Every 1 ms: scale = clamp(scale - gain * (slip_filtered - threshold) * dt, 0.04, 1)
  • Commanded torque: pedal * Tmax * scale, so motor current falls with it (current = torque / gear / Kt)
  • Tire force uses a Pacejka magic formula scaled by the surface friction. The motor torque has a 12 ms lag.

This is the ABS loop mirrored. ABS releases brake pressure when the wheel runs slower than the car. Traction control cuts drive torque when the wheel runs faster than the car. Both hold slip near the peak of the friction curve, where grip is highest.
